What these terms cover

  • Not Sign in with Glofly: signing in with a Glofly account is only for Glofly's own apps (like Gloslate and Tablespin) and isn't offered to other developers.
  • Constellation integrations: API keys and the bots they act as, slash commands and buttons, incoming and outgoing webhooks, and bridges that read or post messages in nebulas a constellation's owner picked.
  • Linked game servers: game servers (for example FiveM, RedM or Minecraft) that check a player's Glofly link, including the Glofly Whitelist resource.

Your keys and secrets

  • You're responsible for everything done with your keys, bots and servers, including by anyone you let use them.
  • If one leaks, reset it straight away (the constellation's Integrations page) and tell us at support@glofly.app if people's information may have been exposed.

People's information

Everything you get from Glofly about a person (their name, username, photo, Glofly ID, email address, roles, game links, presence and messages) is "Glofly data". With Glofly data you must:

  • Tell people. Say plainly (for example in your bot's description, your server's rules or your own privacy policy) what you get from Glofly, why, how long you keep it and how people can have it deleted.
  • Ask for the least you need and use it only to provide the feature people chose your bot or server for. Ask only for the scopes and nebulas it really uses.
  • Respect "no". People can unlink a game server or leave a constellation, and constellation owners can remove your key at any time. When that happens, stop using what you no longer have permission for and delete it within 30 days unless the law requires you to keep it. Do the same when someone asks you to, or when your bot or server shuts down.
  • Keep it safe with reasonable security, and tell affected people (and us) without undue delay if it's exposed.

You must not:

  • sell, rent or trade Glofly data, or give it to data brokers, ad networks or anyone else, except service providers who handle it only for you and under the same rules;
  • use Glofly data for advertising, to build profiles of people, or to track them across other apps and sites;
  • use messages or other content from Glofly to train, fine-tune or evaluate AI or machine-learning models;
  • combine Glofly data with other information to work out who someone is when they haven't told you, or to find, follow or harass anyone;
  • send people email they didn't ask for: use their email address only for your service's own account messages unless they separately agree to more;
  • build anything aimed at children under 13, or try to sign in or link family accounts (Glofly refuses them).

Messages and bridges

  • A key with permission to read messages can only read the nebulas the constellation's owner picked, and Glofly tells everyone in those nebulas that their messages are shared with your bot. Use those messages only to provide that bridge or feature.
  • Don't keep copies of messages longer than the bridge needs, and remove or update them when Glofly tells you a message was edited or deleted, where your service makes that possible.
  • Never read, store or forward direct messages or group chats. The API doesn't offer them; don't try to get them another way.

Bots, webhooks and game servers

  • A bot must say what it is and what it does, and do only what the constellation's owner set it up for. No spam, mass mentions or unasked-for messages.
  • Game servers: use a player's link only to let them in, give them their roles and show their Glofly name in game. Tell players on your server what the link does.
  • Constellation owners are responsible for the integrations they add, and for following these terms and the Terms of Service in their constellation.

Using the API fairly

  • Stay within the rate limits, and don't try to get around them, Lockdown, bans, timeouts, family-account rules or any other limit or security measure.
  • Don't scrape Glofly, collect information about people who haven't used your bot or server, or use the API to copy Glofly or build a competing messenger from its data.
  • Don't make anything that pretends to be Glofly or the Glofly team, asks for people's Glofly passwords, or hides what it is from the people using it.
  • Found a security problem? Email support@glofly.app and give us a fair chance to fix it before you tell anyone. Never test against accounts or constellations that aren't yours.
