Why Glofly

A messenger that's only about your friends.

Most apps want your attention. Glofly just wants you to reach the people you care about, quickly, and then get out of the way.

  • Ads
  • Feeds to scroll
  • Algorithms deciding what you see
  • Selling or sharing your info

That's a promise in our Privacy Policy, not a slogan.

Made for gaming nights

Glofly shows friends what you're playing and keeps your hours in My games. On Windows, a see-through overlay shows your call and messages on top of the game. Push to talk works while you play, and a screen share can send just the game's sound.

Calls in one click

Voice or video with one friend or the whole group. Add people to a call that's already going, ring only the ones you want, and share your screen with sound.

Privacy you control

Turn off read receipts, go Invisible, set quiet hours, and choose who can send you friend requests. Add two-step sign-in, and get an email when a new device signs in. Location data is stripped from every photo and video you send.

Security comes first

Make it yours

Light, dark or match your computer, six accent colors, and your own sounds for messages, calls and friends coming online. Glofly feels warm because you set it up that way.

New · Constellations

Your crew's own corner of the night sky.

Make a Constellation for your gaming crew, your club or your class. Every topic gets its own nebula, and voice nebulas are always open to drop into.

  • Nebulas for every topic, sorted into categories, with replies, polls and pins
  • Voice nebulas to drop into any time, with video, screen share and Watch together. Nobody gets rung.
  • Roles with colors: Owner, Admin and Moderator, plus your own
  • Invite only or public, with the option to approve each new member
  • A dock on your home screen for mentions, pinned nebulas and quick replies

More ways to hang out

Plans, games and little sparks of fun.

Glofly is for the people you actually talk to, so it's full of small ways to spend time together.

Watch together

Paste a link during a call and everyone watches the same moment. Anyone can pause or skip, or keep the controls to yourself. React with fireflies while you watch.

YouTubeTwitchVimeoDailymotion

Polls and mini games

Ask the group which night works, then play tic-tac-toe, a five-letter word guess or trivia right in the chat.

GIFs and stickers

Search GIFs, or turn any photo into a sticker. Glofly cuts out the background for you.

Link previews

Links get a card with a title and picture. Only Glofly's server opens the page, so the site never sees who you are.

Lanterns and fireflies

A lantern message glows and fades away after it's read. Hold a heart to send a swarm of fireflies across the chat.

Glows

A little firefly by a friend's name gets brighter each day you both write. Miss a day and it only dims a little.

Free to hang

Let friends know you're free for the next hour, so they can call you right away.

Birthday glow

Share your birthday with friends if you like (never the year). On the day, there's cake by your name and confetti.

Broadcast now

Go live with your screen or a game, with its sound. Friends see you're live and drop in to watch, even from their phone.

Year in Glow

Your year with friends as a story: busiest day, longest glow, favorite group, top emoji and more.

Invite your friends

Glofly is better with your friends. Bring them along.

Send an invite straight from the app. No codes to copy, no searching for usernames. They join, and you're already friends.

  1. Download Glofly and sign in

    It's free on Windows, Mac and in your browser.

  2. Open Add friend and type their email

    Press Send invite. They get an email from you with a link to join.

  3. They join, you're friends

    The moment they make their account, you're connected and Glofly lets you know.
