Message Friday Squad

Everything your group chat needs

Glofly keeps up with your friends in real time, on every computer you're signed in on.

Chats and group chats

Replies, reactions, edits, read receipts and typing dots. Pin the chats you care about and find anything with Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac).

Groups of up to 25 friends

Voice, video and screen share

Call a friend or ring the whole group. Share your screen or a single window, and join or leave a group call whenever you like.

One click from any chat

Photos, files and emoji

Drag in photos or paste a screenshot. Location data is stripped from every photo, and risky file types are blocked.

Files up to 25 MB
