Glofly for Mac

One download for every Mac, Apple Silicon or Intel. It's an early test version, so macOS asks you to confirm it the first time you open it.

Download for Mac

macOS 12 or newer · Updates aren't automatic on Mac yet, so download again for a newer version.

Opening it the first time

  1. Open the downloaded Glofly .dmg and drag Glofly into Applications.
  2. Open Glofly from Applications. macOS says it can't check it for malicious software. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Glofly, and click Open Anyway.
  4. Click Open Anyway again and enter your Mac password. From now on Glofly opens like any other app.
  5. For calls, allow the microphone and camera when macOS asks. To share your screen, turn Glofly on in Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, then quit and reopen Glofly.

Why the extra step? Glofly isn't registered with Apple yet. That comes before the Mac version is finished.

If macOS says Glofly "is damaged and can't be opened", open Terminal, run this, then open Glofly again:xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Glofly.app
