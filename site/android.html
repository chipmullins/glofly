Glofly for Android

Your chats, calls and Constellations on your Android phone or tablet. Glofly isn't in the Google Play Store yet, so for now you install it straight from here.

Download for Android

Free · Android 8 or newer · Small updates install themselves

Installing

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android. If your browser asks whether to keep the file, tap Download or Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded Glofly file (from the notification, or your Files app → Downloads).
  3. The first time, Android says your browser isn't allowed to install apps. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect asks about an app it hasn't seen before, tap More details → Install anyway.
  5. Open Glofly and sign in, or create an account. You're in.

What Glofly asks to use

Android asks before an app can use these. Each one is only needed for the feature it's for, and you can change them anytime in Settings → Apps → Glofly.

  • Notifications

    Tap Allow so new messages and calls reach you when Glofly is closed. You can reply right from the notification.

  • Microphone and camera

    Asked the first time you join a call, turn on your camera or record a voice message.

  • Full-screen calls

    Lets a call ring on your lock screen like a phone call. If calls only show as a small notification, turn on Full screen notifications for Glofly.

  • Battery

    Some phones (Samsung, Xiaomi and others) put apps to sleep. If messages arrive late, set Glofly's battery use to Unrestricted.

Updates

Most updates download by themselves and apply the next time you open Glofly. Now and then a bigger update needs a new download: Glofly tells you, and you install it from this page over the one you have. Your chats and sign-in stay put.

Coming to Google Play

Glofly is on its way to the Play Store. When it's there, you'll uninstall this version once and install it from Play, then sign in again. Your chats are kept on your account, so nothing is lost.
