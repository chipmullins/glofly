Chats

Every way to say it

Type it, say it, snap it or send the file. Glofly keeps the whole conversation in one tidy place.

  • Replies, reactions and @mentions so nobody loses the thread
  • Voice messages when typing takes too long
  • Photos, videos and files, with location data stripped from photos and videos
  • GIFs, stickers and link previews that keep your address private
  • Polls and mini games: tic-tac-toe, word guess and trivia
  • Pin messages, mark as unread or send later when the timing's better
  • Edit or delete a message after you send it
  • Pin, mute and search your chats (Ctrl+K, or ⌘K on a Mac)

Calls

Hop on a call in one click

Ring one friend or the whole group. The call keeps going while you chat, browse or play.

  • Voice and video, one-on-one or with the group
  • Share your screen or just one window, with its sound
  • Add people to a call that's already going, or ring only some of the group
  • Push to talk, even while a game is in front
  • Game overlay on Windows: your call and messages over the game
  • Join and leave a group call whenever you like
  • Change your mic and speakers without leaving the call

Constellations

A home for every crew you're part of

Make a Constellation for your gaming crew, club or class. Give each topic a nebula and hang out in voice nebulas whenever you like.

  • Text and voice nebulas, sorted into categories
  • Roles with colors and permissions, and moderators to keep it friendly
  • Invite links, a public directory, or approval for new members
  • Notifications your way for each Constellation and nebula
  • My games: favorite games and playtime on your profile
Watch together

Movie night, miles apart

Start a video during any call and everyone sees the same moment. Talk over it like you're on the same couch.

  • YouTube, Twitch, Vimeo and Dailymotion
  • Stays in sync when anyone pauses or skips
  • Only me keeps the controls with you
  • Firefly reactions float over the video
Friends

Know the moment they're on

See who's around, what they're up to, and get a little chime when your favorite people come online.

  • "Notify me when they come online": ring the bell on any friend's profile
  • Glows that grow each day you both write
  • Free to hang tells friends you're free for the next hour
  • Broadcast now: go live and let friends drop in to watch
  • Active on mobile: a tiny phone shows who's on their phone
  • What they're playing and custom status messages
  • Online, Away, Do not disturb or Invisible, your choice

Bring your people with one email

  • No codes, no searching: the invite does the friending
  • You hear about it the second they join
  • Invites last 14 days, and you can send up to 10 a day

Phones and iPad

Your chats, in your pocket

Glofly for iPhone and iPad is in beta: join it with Apple’s TestFlight app. Android is coming soon to Google Play. Same chats, same friends, same calls, wherever you are.

  • Notifications you can reply to without opening the app
  • Calls ring your phone, and keep going with the screen off
  • Voice messages, photos and reactions just like the desktop
  • Picture-in-picture for video calls
  • Constellations and Watch together on the go
  • Two panes on iPad: your chats beside the conversation
Security

Your account stays yours

Security comes first in Glofly. A few switches keep your account safe without getting in your way.

  • Two-step sign-in: a code by email after your password
  • Sign in with your phone: scan a QR code on the computer, no password typed
  • New sign-in emails when a device you haven't used signs in
  • See and sign out every device from Settings
  • Quiet hours silence notifications overnight
