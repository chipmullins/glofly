Delete your Glofly account

Last updated October 11, 2026

The short version

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account, then enter your password.
  • Can't use the app? Email support@glofly.app from the address on your account.
  • Your account is hidden right away and removed for good after 30 days.

In the app

On a computer (Windows or Mac)

  1. Open Glofly and click the gear next to your name, then Settings.
  2. Go to Account and choose Delete account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

On a phone (Android)

  1. Open Glofly and tap Settings at the bottom.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

You're signed out on every device straight away.

Without the app

Email support@glofly.app with the subject "Delete my Glofly account", from the email address your account uses, and include your username. So nobody can delete someone else's account, we'll only act on a request from that address (we may ask you to confirm by replying). We'll delete the account within 7 days and reply when it's done.

Forgot your password? You can also use Forgot password? on the Glofly sign-in screen, then delete the account from Settings.

What gets deleted

  • Right away: your account is hidden from search, friends lists and profiles, nobody can message or call you, and every device is signed out (including phone notifications).
  • After 30 days, for good: your profile, photo, email address, birthday, friends and blocks, your direct messages (for both people) and their photos and files, and the messages, photos and files you sent in groups.
  • Changed your mind? Log in within the 30 days to restore everything.

What's kept, and for how long

  • Reports: if someone reported you, or you reported someone, the report and the copy of the messages it includes are kept for up to a year after it's resolved, so repeated problems can be spotted.
  • Backups: the daily database backups can hold removed information for a short time until they're replaced.
  • Group chats carry on without you; they show that you left.
