Four things to look at before I build them

The rest of the round (faster search, limits that survive restarts, grouped phone notifications, Share to Glofly, iPad shortcuts) doesn't change how Glofly looks, so it's being built now.

6Your invite link

One personal link (glofly.app/i/…) you can paste anywhere. Whoever joins from it, or opens it while signed in, becomes your friend right away. Reset makes the old link stop working. It's in Add friend, the welcome, the empty Friends screen and Settings → Friends.

Two artboards: the card in Add friend, and the page people see.

7Statuses that clear themselves

A "Clear after" row under your custom status: 30 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, Today, This week or Never. The server takes it off on time, and the picker tells you when it ends.

9Role-only nebulas

A nebula (or a whole category) can be for Everyone or Only these roles. People without the role don't see it anywhere. Picking "Valorant" in the welcome unlocks #valorant.

11Templates that set everything up

Each template now also brings starter roles, house rules, a welcome and an accent color, and the picker shows exactly what you'll get. Mostly the wording needs your OK.

Everything follows the system theme and your accent color, like the rest of Glofly. Phones get the same screens in their own layout.
